# ADR 0008 — Rendering is deterministic

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Once the publication model is final, HTML/CSS/PDF rendering and validation are deterministic.

Generative models do not redesign the edition at print time.

## Rationale

Printing requires predictable geometry, failure handling, and reproducibility.

## Consequences

Visual exploration may use generative tools, but approved designs become templates and design tokens.
